# Preliminary results (P1 milestone)

Our first sweep, from the P1 checkpoint. It used an earlier version of the pipeline:
whole camera-trap frames instead of animal crops, a random split (photo bursts could land in
both train and test), post-training quantization only, and simulated sub-8-bit levels.

Kept for reference only. The final results are in `data/results/` and `data/figures/`.
